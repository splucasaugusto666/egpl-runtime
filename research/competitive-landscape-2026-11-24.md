Product decision: prioritize a small independent gate with machine-readable outcomes, a practical integration path and opt-in local model execution. Alternatives: a broad skills collection would duplicate established projects; a new all-in-one harness would be harder to adopt and validate. The selected design is complementary, not an evidence-backed superiority claim.
